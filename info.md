ABOUT ✶
Archive Assistant is a transformative fan utility tool created for fans. This project is built upon a foundation of deep respect for the Organization for Transformative Works (OTW) and the Archive of Our Own (AO3). Its mission is to provide a tool that enhances the personal organization and preservation of fanworks without compromising the integrity of the Archive or the rights of its creators

This tool is intended for personal use only and is intended to function as a companion and not a replacement for the Archive of Our Own.

Fair Use Notice
This webpage and any associated tools facilitate the processing of metadata and fanwork content for purposes such as personal archiving, data organization, and non-commercial research.

To maintain the safety of the Archive and the trust of the community, users are strictly prohibited from the following:

You shall not use this tool to reupload works and data for the purpose of hosting them on other platforms without consent from the original author.
you shall not attempt to bypass rate limiting measures of this tool.
